Our Love Story

God brought us together in His extraordinary way. We didn't come from the same community, the same workplace, or even the same circle of friends.

God introduced us in a place we never would have imagined, yet He allowed it all to happen. Meeting after meeting, argument after argument, until love and trust began to grow between us. Even when we reached the point of wanting to give up, God continued to shape us into the couple He has prepared before Him.
